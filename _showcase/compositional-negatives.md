  <h5 class="mt-0">Can MLLMs Tell Valid from Counterfactual Descriptions?</h5>
  <p class="small text-secondary font-weight-bold mb-1">Computer Vision course project, IIIT Hyderabad &middot; with a labmate</p>
  <p class="small text-muted mb-2">Annotated fine-grained scene graphs of multi-instance scenes and used them to build valid referring expressions and hard negatives that change one object, attribute or relation. Models did better in multiple-choice form, but under a strict paired metric that requires judging both the valid and the counterfactual expression correctly, the best model scored only 16.6%.</p>
