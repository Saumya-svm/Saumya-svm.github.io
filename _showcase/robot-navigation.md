  <h5 class="mt-0">Geometry for Robot Navigation</h5>
  <p class="small text-secondary font-weight-bold mb-1">Ongoing collaboration with <a href="https://oravus.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Sourav Garg</a></p>

This is early-stage exploration with no results yet.</p>
