  <h5 class="mt-0">MIST: Multilingual Incidental Scene Text</h5>
  <p class="small text-secondary font-weight-bold mb-1">WACV 2026</p>
  <p class="small text-muted mb-2">Dataset and benchmark of about 12K road-scene images and 576K annotated text instances across 11 scripts, captured without any intent to photograph text. Introduces metrics for how incidental a dataset is and systematically evaluates detector robustness. Led data curation, annotation, baseline evaluation and robustness analysis.</p>
