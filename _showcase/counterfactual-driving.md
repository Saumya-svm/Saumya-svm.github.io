  <h5 class="mt-0">Counterfactual Referring in Driving Scenes</h5>
  <p class="small text-secondary font-weight-bold mb-1">Responsible and Safe AI Systems course project, IIIT Hyderabad</p>
  <p class="small text-muted mb-2">Two counterfactual referring-expression tasks on RADBench-AD: switch to a similar object when the description changes, or reject a description that matches nothing, scored with a paired consistency metric. General-purpose MLLMs switched referents more reliably but struggled to reject invalid references; the driving-specific UniDriveVLA showed the opposite pattern. Led implementation, experiments and analysis.</p>
