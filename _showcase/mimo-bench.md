  <h5 class="mt-0">MIMO-Bench: Visual Reasoning in Dense Scenes</h5>
  <p class="small text-secondary font-weight-bold mb-1">arXiv preprint, 2026</p>
  <p class="small text-muted mb-2">3,802 questions on attribute binding and numerical counting in scenes with many instances of several object categories. Studies how similar-looking distractors and compositional referring expressions affect MLLMs, and finds that targeted visual cropping helps more than chain-of-thought. On real images, the best model reaches 52.8% on attribute binding against 89.3% for humans.</p>
